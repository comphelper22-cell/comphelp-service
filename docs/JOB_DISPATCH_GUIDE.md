# Job Dispatch Guide

Job Dispatch manages the operational flow from customer approval to completed work.

## Workflow

Customer -> Create Estimate -> Approve Estimate -> Create Job -> Assign Technician -> Schedule Job -> Update Status -> Complete Job -> Generate Invoice Placeholder -> Timeline Updated -> AI Recommendation.

## Marketplace UI

Open Marketplace Manager and choose `Job Dispatch`.

Available actions:

- Save Job
- Assign
- Schedule
- Complete
- Refresh Jobs
- AI Dispatch

## Dispatch Board

The Dispatch Board lists jobs with job number, service, customer, assigned technician, and schedule status.

## Timeline

Each job records operational events such as creation, assignment, schedule changes, status changes, completion, invoice placeholder generation, and AI recommendations.
