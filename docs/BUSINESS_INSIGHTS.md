# Business Insights

The AI Operations Assistant generates practical business insights from existing data.

## Business Health

The health score starts at `100` and subtracts risk points for:

- Overdue invoices
- Emergency jobs
- Waiting-parts jobs
- Inactive customers
- Scheduling conflicts

Health statuses:

- `healthy`
- `watch`
- `needs_attention`

## Revenue Insights

Revenue insight fields:

- Revenue today
- Revenue this month
- Outstanding balance
- Outstanding invoices
- Overdue invoices
- Paid invoices
- Estimate conversion rate
- Top paying customers

## Job Insights

Job insight fields:

- Today's jobs
- Emergency jobs
- Waiting-parts jobs
- Completed jobs today
- Technician workload
- Overloaded technicians
- Scheduling conflicts
- Recommended technician

## Customer Insights

Customer insight fields:

- Active customers
- New customers this week
- Customers without recent contact
- Customers with overdue invoices
- Customers with active jobs

## Recommendations

The assistant currently recommends:

- Follow up unpaid invoices
- Send reminders for overdue balances
- Call customers with approved estimates
- Confirm emergency dispatch
- Check waiting-parts jobs
- Review overloaded technicians
- Call inactive customers
- Suggest relevant upsells
