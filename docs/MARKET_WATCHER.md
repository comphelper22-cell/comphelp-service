# Market Watcher

The Market Watcher tracks local market signals that can help CompHelp Service choose better campaigns, service offers, and customer segments.

## Tracked Signals

- Competitor offers
- Common prices
- Popular services
- Local demand
- Seasonal opportunities
- Weak competitor review themes
- New businesses opening
- Businesses with bad technology or security review signals

## Current Data Mode

The current version uses safe manual public-research placeholders. It does not connect to Google, Yelp, Facebook, Craigslist, or paid data sources.

## Output

Market Watcher returns:

- Competitor alerts
- Popular services
- Local demand summaries
- Seasonal opportunities
- Weak review signals
- New business signals

## Recommended Use

Use Market Watcher to decide:

- Which service to promote this week.
- Which city or neighborhood to focus on.
- Which content topic to publish.
- Which customer segment deserves owner-reviewed outreach.
