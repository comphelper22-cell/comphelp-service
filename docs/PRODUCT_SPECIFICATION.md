# Product Specification

## Product

CompHelp AI is an AI Business Operating System for service businesses.

## Users

- Owner.
- Manager.
- Dispatcher.
- Technician.
- Customer.
- Viewer.

## Core Jobs To Be Done

- Capture leads.
- Qualify customers.
- Create estimates.
- Select vendors.
- Track projects.
- Follow up with customers.
- Create marketing drafts.
- Publish SEO pages.
- Track revenue and commissions.
- Validate and deploy software safely.

## Product Modules

Lead Manager: create, qualify, and track leads.

CRM: manage customers, notes, statuses, and activity.

Estimate Manager: create low, high, and recommended quote ranges.

Vendor Manager: store and rank partners.

Dispatcher: recommend vendor or internal technician.

Project Manager: track job status, completion, media, and reviews.

Gallery Manager: organize media and publish job cards.

SMM Manager: create posts, captions, hashtags, scripts, and schedules.

SEO Manager: create city pages, service pages, blog ideas, and metadata.

Developer Center: validate, report, and prepare deployment.

Business Dashboard: summarize KPIs.

## Product Principles

- Human approval for risky actions.
- Fast lead response.
- Clear estimates.
- Privacy-safe media.
- Local SEO readiness.
- Vendor accountability.
- Deployment safety.

## Success Metrics

- Leads captured.
- Response time.
- Quote conversion.
- Close rate.
- Revenue.
- Profit margin.
- Commission tracked.
- Completed projects.
- Approved content.
- Validation pass rate.
