# CRM User Guide

The Real Customer CRM manages customer records inside Marketplace Manager.

## Customer Actions

- Create Customer
- Edit Customer
- Delete Customer
- Archive Customer
- Restore Customer
- Search Customers
- Open Customer Details
- Add Customer Notes

## Customer Fields

- Full Name
- Company
- Phone
- Email
- Address
- City
- State
- ZIP
- Notes
- Status
- Tags
- Lead Source
- Assigned Sales
- Assigned Technician
- Created Date
- Updated Date

## Search and Filters

Search covers name, phone, email, company, status, and tags.

Filters include active, archived, new, returning, commercial, and residential.

## Timeline

The CRM timeline combines customer events, estimates, jobs, invoices, payments, notes, and AI recommendation events when available.

## Notes

Notes support pinned and internal flags. Deleted notes are soft-deleted.
