# Outreach Policy

CompHelp Service uses a conservative outreach policy to avoid spam and protect customer trust.

## Core Rules

- Never auto-send cold outreach.
- Every outreach draft requires owner approval.
- Do not contact opted-out contacts.
- Do not message the same lead twice within seven days unless they replied.
- Do not use deceptive language or false urgency.
- Do not imply a human personally researched a lead if the system generated the draft.
- Do not scrape private personal data.
- Keep outreach short, useful, and respectful.

## Draft Limits

The default cold outreach draft limit is 10 per day.

## Allowed Drafts

The AI Marketing Manager may prepare:

- Call script
- SMS draft
- Email draft
- Instagram DM draft

These drafts are not sent automatically.

## SMS Compliance

SMS drafts include an opt-out line where appropriate, such as `Reply STOP to opt out.`

## Approval Status

Supported statuses:

- draft
- needs_review
- approved
- rejected
- queued
- sent
- replied
- opted_out

Current production behavior is draft-only unless the owner explicitly approves future sending workflows.
