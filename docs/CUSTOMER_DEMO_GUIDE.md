# Customer Demo Guide

## Before Demo

- Run `npm run check-project`.
- Confirm the dashboard loads.
- Confirm Beta Center is visible.
- Review known limitations.
- Prepare a short customer-specific example.

## During Demo

- Keep the demo under 10 minutes.
- Use the Beta Center as the starting point.
- Let the customer ask questions.
- Avoid technical implementation details unless requested.
- Do not claim external integrations are live.

## After Demo

- Record feedback.
- Identify the customer’s most valuable workflow.
- Note blockers.
- Ask if they want to join the beta waitlist.

## Demo Positioning

CompHelp AI is not only a CRM. It is a business operating dashboard that coordinates sales, operations, finance, dispatch, marketing, analytics, and AI recommendations.
