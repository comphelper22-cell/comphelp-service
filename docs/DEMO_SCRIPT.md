# Demo Script

## Opening

“CompHelp AI is an AI business operating system for service businesses. In this demo, we’ll use a synthetic service company so you can see how the platform helps an owner understand leads, jobs, money, marketing, and next actions quickly.”

## Minute 1: Beta Center

Show the welcome wizard, demo company, readiness score, and known limitations.

## Minutes 2-3: Founder Command Center

Show business health, top AI actions, revenue opportunities, risks, and workflow activity.

## Minutes 4-5: Sales and Operations

Show Sales Manager for lead and estimate follow-up. Then show Operations Center for today’s jobs and urgent work.

## Minutes 6-7: Dispatch, Finance, Analytics

Show Dispatch AI suggestions, Finance Center, and Analytics & Reports.

## Minute 8: Growth

Show Marketing & Growth Center for lead sources, SEO, reviews, and campaign ideas.

## Minute 9: Limitations

Explain that external services, payment processing, and public integrations are not connected in beta demo mode.

## Minute 10: Feedback

Ask:

- What was clear?
- What was confusing?
- What would you use first?
- What feature is missing?
- Would you want to test this with your business?
