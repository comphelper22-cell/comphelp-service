# Product Strategy Review

CompHelp AI should become the operating system for service businesses before becoming a broad agent marketplace.

## Near-Term Focus

- Supabase readiness.
- CRM v2.
- Estimate engine accuracy.
- Dispatcher and vendor workflows.
- Customer feedback loop.
- Quality gates.

## Strategy Rules

- Do not add automation before the data model is stable.
- Do not publish external claims without evidence.
- Do not expand into too many verticals before the first service workflow is strong.
- Keep owner approval in all risky loops.

## Strategic Bet

Small service businesses need one system that connects leads, estimates, jobs, vendors, follow-ups, marketing, and reporting. CompHelp AI should own that workflow.
