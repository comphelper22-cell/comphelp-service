# Beta Launch Plan

## Mission

Prepare CompHelp AI for first customer demonstrations and controlled beta testing.

## Demo Goal

A new customer should understand the platform in less than 10 minutes:

1. What the product does.
2. Who it helps.
3. How it improves daily service business operations.
4. What is working now.
5. What is still a known limitation.

## Beta Audience

- Local service business owners.
- Dispatchers.
- Small teams that manage leads, jobs, estimates, invoices, marketing, and follow-up manually.

## Demo Flow

1. Open Beta Center.
2. Explain the demo company and synthetic demo customers.
3. Walk through Founder Command Center.
4. Show Sales Manager and Operations Center.
5. Show Dispatch AI, Finance Center, and Analytics.
6. Show known limitations.
7. Capture feedback.

## Success Criteria

- Customer can explain the value back in their own words.
- Customer identifies at least one workflow they would use.
- Customer understands the system is in beta.
- Customer gives feedback on missing features or confusion.

## Beta Rules

- Do not connect external services during demo.
- Do not show private customer data.
- Do not present demo data as real results.
- Do not promise features that are not implemented.
- Capture all feedback before offering next steps.
