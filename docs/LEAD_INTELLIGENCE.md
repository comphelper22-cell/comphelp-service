# Lead Intelligence

Lead Intelligence identifies potential business opportunities using public-source placeholders and owner-reviewed research workflows.

## Supported Source Types

- Google Business public listings placeholder
- Yelp public business categories placeholder
- Facebook and Marketplace manual research placeholder
- Craigslist services placeholder
- Local business directories placeholder
- Construction, remodeling, and service business listing placeholder

No external source is scraped automatically in the current version.

## Target Categories

- Small offices
- Dental offices
- Restaurants
- Retail stores
- Warehouses
- Apartment managers
- Property managers
- Camera installation prospects
- WiFi and network cabling prospects
- Computer repair prospects
- Data recovery prospects

## Lead Fields

- Source
- Business name
- Business type
- City
- Neighborhood
- Possible service need
- Reason this is a lead
- Public listing URL placeholder
- Review signals
- Website quality
- Urgency
- Likely budget
- Distance
- Service fit
- Score
- Probability to close
- Status

## CRM Handoff

Selected leads may be saved into CRM as:

- Prospect
- Lead
- Warm Lead
- Cold Lead
- Follow-up Required

The CRM record includes a pinned internal note that outreach requires owner approval.
